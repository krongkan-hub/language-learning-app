## Status

The project is a CLI language-learning roleplay application using local LLMs via MLX on Apple Silicon for English and Japanese instruction.

All figures below were re-measured on **2026-08-15**. Every deterministic gate is green.

The headline number to read here is the coach's **63.0%**, and it should not be read as a regression. The coach suite was expanded from 16 cases to 46 that day specifically to find out how much Japanese it was missing, because 6 Japanese fixtures were not enough to answer that. The answer is: a lot. See OPEN-10 — this is now the largest known quality gap in the product.

| [`playtest_sample.py`](scripts/playtest_sample.py) | 99.5% (199/200, seed 42) | last full run; its 1 failure (Sc28 task 11) is fixed and re-playtested 3/3 — OPEN-09 |

**Read the 99.5% narrowly.** The playtest measures *task completion* — whether a simulated learner can get the NPC to the goal, graded by the judge. It says nothing about whether the learner's language was corrected, so it is blind to the coach by construction. "99.5% of tasks are winnable" and "the coach misses ~3/4 of Japanese errors" (OPEN-10) are both true at the same time, and only the first one looks like a headline. Nothing in the suite table above measures whether a learner actually improves.

No fixture was deleted, reworded, or relabelled to reach these numbers. [`eval/eval_baselines.json`](eval/eval_baselines.json) floors each suite below its measured score, because every suite samples only a handful of iterations and a single flaky run is normal — the actor read 96 / 98 / 99 per 100 across three consecutive runs with no code change. A real regression moves more than one run.

The actor expansion (4 → 20 cases) is the mirror image of the coach one: the score went **up**, so the old 4 cases were thin rather than wrong, and no new failure class appeared. The value was in what it surfaced outside the score — OPEN-12.

| OPEN-10 | Coach misses ~3/4 of Japanese learner errors | [`app/coach.py`](app/coach.py), [`eval/coach_cases.json`](eval/coach_cases.json) | **Open — largest known quality gap.** Measured 2026-08-15 by expanding the coach suite from 16 cases to 46 (Japanese 6 → 36, including 9 new `clean` cases). Blind classes: particle choice 1/7 (を/に, に/で, で/に, に/と, は/が), transitive/intransitive **0/3** (`電気をつきました`, `会議が始めました`, `窓が閉めました`), register 1/3 (`先生、俺は宿題を忘れた`), counters 1/3, word order 1/3. **The failure is pure under-detection, not over-correction: all 12 clean cases pass 5/5.** The coach answers "Perfectly natural!" to real mistakes. Localization happens once at the end of `coach_feedback`, after the particle net, so `COACH_SYS` is untouched; score and clean 12/12 both unchanged. |
